# Search evidence and editorial limits

The 200 additional queries were checked against fresh US/en desktop Google
organic results on September 12, 2026. Each campaign has one forty-query Apify
run, with compact results in its `serp-<campaign>.json` file. All 200 queries
returned organic results. That fact **does not prove search volume, customer
demand, commercial viability, or an achievable ranking position**.

The fifty-per-ICP quota is the requested review-library size, not an empirical
finding that every campaign has exactly fifty equally valuable opportunities.
Do not publish all 250 simply to meet that quota. Paid demand enrichment and
human selection remain separate from this manual writing batch.

## Topic review decisions

- Removed generic fundraising metrics/finance-slide topics from the accelerator
  expansion and replaced them with specific recording, product proof, playback,
  sound, screen-sharing and accessibility-demonstration jobs.
- Replaced two cross-campaign overlaps: investor booth loops duplicated the GTM
  trade-show loop, and investor subtitle translation duplicated localization.
  The replacements cover mobile-app projection and a real screen-reader demo.
- Replaced several generic founder website/email topics with practical founder
  recording tasks. PR/media-presence topics remain relevant to the funded-founder
  campaign, but their connection to a video product is less direct.
- Replaced non-video GTM infographic/white-paper/ebook topics with frame extraction,
  multi-speaker panel editing and modular sales videos.
- Shortened many internally phrased queries. Portfolio articles adapt broader
  operational searches to a multi-company media context; the broad query alone
  does not demonstrate portfolio-operator intent.

## Publication prioritization still needed

Founder PR/contributed-article topics can attract a different audience from video
buyers. Portfolio topics such as learning paths, cost allocation and needs
assessment have substantial non-media intent. Product-comparison queries can be
dominated by tools, vendor documentation or service pages rather than editorial
guides. These are reasons for later selection or revision, not reasons to invent
better metrics or claim that competitors omitted a workflow without reading them.

Every article's private notes distinguish its editorial gap hypothesis and
actual SERP competitors. FAQs only count as observed PAA when their exact wording
appears in a collected record; other questions are marked editorial. Public copy
does not display research-run or compliance boilerplate.

The existing fifty articles keep their original evidence and source notes
unchanged. This expansion does not retroactively upgrade their validation status.
