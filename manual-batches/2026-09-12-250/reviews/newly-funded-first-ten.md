Required author correction: add a compact existing-outreach inventory (recipient, material/angle/access already offered or shared, accepted terms/timing, current status). Work through one fictional late-exclusive request: determine what is still genuinely unoffered; disclose the relevant existing outreach; offer a clearly available narrower component if acceptable, or decline the incompatible request.
