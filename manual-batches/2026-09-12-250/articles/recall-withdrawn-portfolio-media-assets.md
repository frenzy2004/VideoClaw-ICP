Recall a withdrawn portfolio media asset by identifying the exact released version, mapping its known destinations and coordinating removal or replacement with each owner. Stop additional distribution while the scope is clarified. Track confirmed actions and unresolved copies separately, because revoking a library link does not establish that every published or downloaded copy has disappeared.

## Confirm what is being withdrawn

Ask the company to identify the asset and explain the requested operational action. Does it want a particular version removed, all versions of an interview withdrawn or only a specific quotation replaced? Record the instruction and the contact who can clarify it.

Use the asset ID and release record to avoid recalling the wrong material. Similar filenames or reused thumbnails can make a request ambiguous. If the company sends a screenshot, match it to the actual publication and media version before acting on unrelated files.

Separate the requested action from the reason. The coordinator may need to know that a customer quotation must no longer be used without recording sensitive details about the underlying relationship. Keep the information necessary to perform the task.

If the situation requires legal, privacy or other specialist handling, use the established route. The media operator's work is to contain distribution and coordinate verified actions, not decide the company's rights or obligations.

## Stop the asset from spreading further

Pause scheduled uses and remove the asset from active selection while the recall is underway. Tell the relevant editors which ID is affected so they do not retrieve another copy from an older project folder.

Distinguish withdrawal from deletion. You may need a controlled record of the released version to identify copies and understand what was changed. Follow the organization's actual instructions about retaining that record.

[Google's shared-drive guidance](https://support.google.com/a/users/answer/12380484?hl=en) describes multiple access paths and restrictions. Check the actual grants and links involved rather than assuming that moving a file or changing one broad setting removes every route.

Label the asset's state clearly in the internal library. A note such as “withdrawn from further distribution; replacement pending” gives editors an action. A generic archive label may not explain that the company has specifically asked for withdrawal.

Notify people responsible for imminent uses directly. A status change in a library may not reach someone preparing a newsletter from a downloaded folder.

## Map the destinations you know

Build a destination register before declaring the task complete. Start with the release record and expand through the people who distributed the asset.

| Destination | Responsible party | Possible action | Completion evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fund article | Platform editor | Remove or replace media | Checked public page |
| Shared resource portal | Library manager | Revoke access or remove listing | Tested link and navigation |
| Company social post | Company contact | Apply the company's chosen correction | Company confirmation |
| Partner page | Partner contact | Request removal or replacement | Partner confirmation or unresolved state |
| Downloaded package | Known recipient | Request the specified action | Recipient response, if obtained |

This is an original coordination table. It does not imply that recipients must take a particular legal action or that all copies can be found.

Record the exact URL where available, the version used and any companion text affected. A video may be removed while its old quotation remains in the article introduction. The recall should address the company's actual instruction, including related captions or thumbnails when relevant.

Keep unknown destinations visible as uncertainty. Do not convert “no further copies found” into “no further copies exist.”

## Work through a fictional recall

Alder asks the platform to withdraw interview version three because the company no longer wants one customer quotation distributed. The platform confirms that the request covers the video excerpt and the same quotation in accompanying copy.

The release record shows a fund article, a resource-portal link and a partner newsletter. The editor pauses a scheduled social post, removes the excerpt from the fund article and updates the text. The library manager disables the known portal route and tests it from a recipient view.

[Canto's expiry documentation](https://support.canto.com/hc/en-us/articles/23001802813841-Set-Expiration-Dates) illustrates how a library can remove access through its portals. That kind of controlled action is one part of a recall, not evidence about the partner newsletter or earlier downloads.

The partner confirms that its web archive has been updated but cannot change copies already delivered by email. The register records that limitation. The platform reports the controlled destinations as corrected and the delivered email copies as outside its ability to recall.

A replacement interview can enter the normal release process. It should not be rushed into the old destinations without its own company decision.

## Verify removal and replacement separately

For a removal, check that the affected media is no longer available through the route the user actually followed. Inspect the page, its embedded media and the direct link where the instruction and system behavior require it.

For a replacement, check the new asset's identity, companions and surrounding copy. A replacement file with the same public title can still contain the withdrawn passage if the wrong export was uploaded.

Record the time of the check and the result. Avoid relying solely on a task marked complete by the person who changed the file. A brief recipient-view check can reveal a stale embed or an overlooked thumbnail.

Where another party controls the destination, record what that party confirmed. Distinguish a request sent from a removal completed. If confirmation does not arrive, keep the item unresolved and decide who will follow up.

Do not promise universal disappearance. The report should describe the specific actions performed and the limits of what was checked.

## Close the recall with a usable record

Send the company a concise status note: the affected asset, actions completed, destinations awaiting confirmation and any known limitations. Include the replacement status if one is being prepared.

Preserve the relationship between the withdrawn version and its successor. Future editors should understand why an old link should not be reused. A disconnected replacement file can allow the withdrawn copy to reappear through search.

Review how the asset reached each destination. If several copies lacked release references, improve the distribution register. If a scheduled post escaped the pause, clarify which queues the platform controls and how their owners are notified.

Keep the lesson specific. A recall caused by missing destination records needs better distribution traceability, not necessarily another round of generic pre-publication approval.

The recall is complete when the agreed controlled actions are verified and remaining uncertainty is explicitly handed to the appropriate owner. An honest record of a bounded result is more useful than a claim that every copy has vanished.
