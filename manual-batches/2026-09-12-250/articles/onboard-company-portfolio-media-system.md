Onboard a portfolio company to your media system by introducing the right people, establishing a minimal company record and helping the team complete one real support request. Access alone is not activation. The company should understand what help exists, which facts it owns and how to obtain a useful result without relying on informal introductions.

## Start with the company's next media decision

A new portfolio company may be preparing a launch, hiring its first marketer or simply trying to correct an outdated public description. Begin by asking what it needs to accomplish next. Avoid presenting a long catalog of resources before understanding whether any of them address its immediate work.

The purpose of the first conversation is to connect one real need to an available service. Ask who currently handles media, what material already exists and which upcoming public moment matters. If the company has no immediate production need, choose a useful administrative outcome such as confirming its portfolio directory record.

[a16z's description of portfolio media services](https://a16z.com/what-is-new-media-in-2026/) emphasizes coordinating capabilities around what a company is trying to accomplish. A smaller platform can apply that principle without promising the same services or scale.

Do not assume a funding announcement is the first appropriate task. The company may have already announced, may not be ready to discuss the investment publicly or may need practical support unrelated to fundraising. Its actual situation should determine the first exercise.

## Establish a minimal company record

Create a stable company identifier and record its approved public name, website, primary media contact and backup contact. Add the person responsible for confirming company facts and the route for time-sensitive corrections. These roles may belong to the same person at a small startup, but their functions remain different.

Separate public facts from internal coordination notes. A public company description should not contain the platform's assessment of the founder or private fundraising context. Internal notes should explain how to work together, not become a second promotional profile.

Ask the company to confirm the record. Highlight fields that remain unknown instead of completing them from memory or guessing based on a similar name. When the company has an existing approved description, link to it before drafting another version.

Record where the authoritative material lives and when the company last checked it. The platform does not need to copy every file into its own storage to establish a useful record. For some assets, a maintained company link is the appropriate starting point.

Keep the intake short enough that the company can complete it during real work. Extra fields can be added when an actual service requires them.

## Introduce the service through one task

Choose a first task with a visible completion condition. A company profile correction is complete when the approved change appears in the correct destination. A library retrieval task is complete when the company can open the intended asset and understands its stated use. An outline review is complete when the company receives actionable comments on the supplied draft.

[Atlassian's marketing service template](https://www.atlassian.com/software/jira/templates/marketing-service-management) connects a request portal with tailored request types and tracking. Your onboarding should teach that route through use, rather than relying on a presentation about the system.

Let the company contact submit the request. If staff complete every step on their behalf, the platform cannot tell whether the route is understandable. Stay available to help, and note confusing labels or missing explanations.

Return an acknowledgement that shows the chosen service, supplied material and next decision. Explain whether the request has merely arrived or has been accepted. That distinction prevents the first interaction from creating an unintended production promise.

## Use an activation map

The following fictional example follows a company called Alder as it joins an existing media-support program.

| Stage | Alder's action | Platform action | Evidence of progress |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Introduction | Names its marketing lead | Explains available profile support | Shared understanding of the first task |
| Company record | Confirms its public description | Creates the company ID and contacts | Confirmed record |
| Access | Opens the relevant resource area | Resolves the correct access route | Successful retrieval |
| First request | Submits a directory correction | Accepts the bounded change | Request reference and scope |
| Completion | Checks the corrected listing | Records the result and next contact | Company confirmation |

Suppose Alder can sign in but cannot find the correction request. The access stage is complete, but activation is not. Improve the navigation or explain the route; do not mark the company fully onboarded because the invitation was accepted.

If Alder has no approved replacement description, pause the correction task and identify who will supply it. Do not publish a platform-written guess to make the onboarding tracker look finished.

This map works because each stage has observable evidence. A sent email or an offered meeting is an activity, not proof that the company understands the service.

## Avoid transferring the company's responsibilities

Explain what the platform can check and what the company must confirm. The platform can identify inconsistent wording, missing source references or an unclear audience. The company remains the source for its current product facts, authorized quotations and release decisions.

Make this practical through the first task. When reviewing a profile, ask the company to confirm the factual sentence that changed. When locating an asset, show the supplied release instruction. Avoid burying those responsibilities in a long policy document that nobody connects to the work.

Introduce the backup contact before there is an urgent deadline. Confirm which kinds of decisions that person can handle. A backup who can relay a message may not be able to approve a public company statement.

Also establish the route for leaving the service or changing contacts. Companies grow, marketers move and responsibilities shift. The onboarding record should make future updates straightforward, rather than anchoring the relationship permanently to the person who happened to join first.

Do not request credentials or broad access to company systems merely to make onboarding convenient. Ask for the minimum material necessary for the accepted media task.

## Close onboarding with a useful handoff

Send a short completion note containing the company record location, the request route, the next relevant resource and the contact for problems. Refer to the task the company just completed so the note feels connected to actual work.

Ask the company contact to explain how they would make a second request. This can be a brief conversation, not a test. If they still expect to message an investment partner for routine assistance, the support route has not become clear enough.

Record unresolved items separately. A company can successfully use profile support while another service remains unavailable or requires more preparation. Avoid one overall “done” label that hides those distinctions.

Review the first request internally. Identify whether the company struggled with service selection, input preparation, access or understanding the result. Those observations can improve the onboarding experience for the next company.

A good introduction leaves the company with a working relationship and a completed task. The portal, contact list and welcome message support that outcome; none of them substitutes for it.
