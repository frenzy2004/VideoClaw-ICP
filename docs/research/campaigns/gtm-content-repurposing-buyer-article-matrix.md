## Editorial boundary

This matrix turns the approved campaign research into exactly ten clusters with five non-overlapping intent angles per cluster. Competitor pages are evidence only of their publishers' current claims and category coverage, while official platform, accessibility, consumer-protection, copyright, and analytics documentation supports factual process guidance; every proposed framework, scorecard, visual, sequence, or CTA remains a VideoClaw editorial recommendation until tested.

VideoClaw should be described, where context requires it, only as a private-alpha, prompt-based Mac app for agentic video content creation. The matrix does not claim unverified pricing, access, input or output limits, integrations, security, privacy, rights, throughput, quality, customer outcomes, or parity between public app and CLI documentation.
