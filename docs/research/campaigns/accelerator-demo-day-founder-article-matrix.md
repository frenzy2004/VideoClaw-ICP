## Research controls

This matrix turns the approved Campaign 2 research into exactly 50 article specifications across ten required clusters. The five angles within each cluster are intentionally non-overlapping, and the timeline specifications use first-party examples from accelerator and startup ecosystems beyond San Francisco—including Cambridge/Boston, New York, Austin, Chicago, Boulder, Columbus, Los Angeles/Southern California, and New Orleans—without inventing city or cohort variants.

Primary keywords are editorial hypotheses, not claims of search volume, keyword difficulty, CPC, traffic, or rankability. Any VideoClaw mention must preserve the current product-truth boundary: private-alpha Mac app, prompt-based agentic video content creation, and only the use cases visibly named on VideoClaw's public pages; no specification below assumes deck ingestion, automated investor outreach, analytics, turnaround, output format, privacy terms, or business outcomes.

Every entry in the competitor/content-gap column is a **gap hypothesis pending observed US Apify SERP validation**, not a claim about the current search-result category. A hypothesis can become a selected opportunity only after its article record carries the observed query, ranking competitors, run ID, dataset ID, result count, and review date.
