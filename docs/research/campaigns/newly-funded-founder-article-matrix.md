# Newly Funded Founder Article Matrix

**Campaign:** `newly-funded-founder`

**Audience:** Newly funded US startup founder who needs a credible media presence and a GTM content plan

**Research checked:** 2026-09-01
**Status:** Editorial specifications; not publication-ready articles

## Evidence and keyword policy

This matrix extends the existing VideoClaw US SEO/AEO/GEO research pack with current primary-source checks. Primary and secondary keywords are editorial search-intent hypotheses, not claims of demand: no volume, difficulty, CPC, traffic, customer result, or ranking estimate is supplied because no authenticated provider or first-party performance account was available.

The ten clusters cover different founder jobs. Within every cluster, the five specifications use non-overlapping intent angles; across the full matrix, every article ID, primary keyword, title, and slug is unique. Product-oriented CTAs are recommendations only and must be reconciled with current VideoClaw availability, capability, privacy, and offer terms before publication. Every CTA that mentions a worksheet, template, canvas, card, scorecard, checklist, or diagram refers to the owned in-article asset planned in that specification's `owned_editorial_media_brief`; it does not claim that a separate external asset already exists.
