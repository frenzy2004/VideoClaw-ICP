**Campaign:** `video-production-comparison`

**Audience:** A United States startup founder comparing an agency, freelancer, internal editor or team, and AI video agent

**Status:** Editorial specifications; keyword metrics and quantified provider comparisons remain unvalidated

## Research and publication guardrails

This matrix follows the VideoClaw Markdown content-engine design and the existing US SEO/AEO/GEO research pack. Its primary keywords are search-intent hypotheses, not claims of volume, difficulty, cost per click, traffic, or ranking potential; those fields must remain pending until authenticated provider data is imported.

The source set favors current government guidance, standards bodies, and first-party product documentation. A source verifies only the fact it actually states: every proposed framework, threshold, scoring weight, worksheet, diagram, or CTA is an editorial recommendation until VideoClaw publishes a dated methodology and, where relevant, a same-brief benchmark.

Each cluster uses five non-overlapping intent angles. Titles, slugs, primary keywords, and article IDs are unique across all 50 specifications.

Every competitor/content-gap cell below is explicitly a hypothesis pending observed US Google evidence from Apify. Category-wide wording must not be published as an observed finding until the selected article records include the supporting query, ranking competitors, run ID, and dataset ID.
